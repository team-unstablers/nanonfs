# 2026-05-09 — RFC 5531 reference audit (follow-up to the 7530 audit)

- Driven by `llm_prompts/20260509-audit-rfc-reference.rfc5531.xml`. Same shape as the RFC 7530 audit earlier in the day, but applied to every `RFC 5531 §X` / `Appendix X` citation in the repo against `docs/rfc5531.txt`. Full table of corrections in `llm_prompts/20260509-audit-rfc-reference.rfc5531.execution-log.20260509.xml`.
- Three defects, all in doc-comments only (no behavior change):
  - `Sources/NanoNFS/RPC/RPCMessage.swift:24` — AUTH_SYS `authsys_parms` cited as `Appendix A.1`. RFC 5531's Appendix A has **no sub-numbering**; the struct is in plain `Appendix A`.
  - `Sources/NanoNFS/RPC/RPCMessage.swift:152` — "AUTH_NONE is what AUTH_SYS expects for replies" cited as `§8.2`. §8.2 only defines the generic `opaque_auth` envelope; the AUTH_SYS-specific reply-verifier flavor rule lives in **Appendix A** ("The flavor value of the verifier received in the reply message from the server may be 'AUTH_NONE' or 'AUTH_SHORT'").
  - `Sources/NanoNFS/RPC/RPCConstants.swift:36` — `auth_stat` cited as `§A.2`. `§A.2` does not exist in RFC 5531 at all; `auth_stat` is defined in **§9** (RPC Message Protocol) right next to `accept_stat`/`reject_stat`. Fixed citation also makes this enum's annotation consistent with the surrounding `§9` annotations on `RPCAcceptStatus`/`RPCRejectStatus`.
- Everything else (`§9` for `rpc_msg`/`xid`/program identification, `§8.2` for `opaque_auth`/`auth_flavor`, `§11` for record marking) verified correct and left as-is.
- Pattern-wise, two of the three defects are the same "invented appendix sub-section" failure mode (`Appendix A.1`, `§A.2`); the third is "pinned to the closest-feeling chapter" (§8.2 vs Appendix A for AUTH_SYS reply-verifier semantics). Worth keeping next to the §16-offset trap from the 7530 audit when reasoning about future RFC citations. The §4 anti-hallucination rule — "look the section up directly in the RFC text before writing the citation" — generalizes verbatim to RFC 5531; no separate policy needed.
- `swift build` green after the edits.
